How to make a Dombolo

These are soft, fluffy steamed dumplings, perfect for soaking up stew or curry.

Ingredients

  • 500 g bread flour (or all-purpose)
  • 2 tsp instant yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 300 ml warm water

Steps

  1. Combine 500 g bread flour or all-purpose flour, 2 tsp instant yeast, 1 tsp sugar and 1 tsp salt in a large bowl. Add 1 tbsp oil and most of the 300 ml warm water, mixing until a soft dough forms. Add a splash more water if it's dry.
  2. Knead on a lightly floured surface until smooth and elastic.
  3. Place in an oiled bowl, cover with a damp cloth and leave somewhere warm until doubled.
  4. Punch down the dough and shape into 6 smooth balls. PLace them in a greased plate or in a greased pot lid-friendly dish and cover. Let them puff up.
  5. Bring about 3 cm of water to a gentle simmer in a large pot. Set the dombolo on a heatproof plate or trivet above the water. Cover with a tight lid and steam without lifting the lid.
  6. Check that a skewer comes out clean and the dombolo are springy. Serve hot with stew, curry or gravy.

NOTES

Keep the lid on while steaming or they may collapse. Make sure the water never boils dry. A tea towel under the lid stops drips fro falling on the dough. Traditionally dombolo are cooked right on top of a simmering stew, which flavours them.

This recipe makes 6 servings.
