How to make Traditional Beer

A thick, tangy, lightly alcoholic beer made from maize meal and sorghum malt.

Ingredients

  • 500 g maize meal
  • 2500 ml water
  • 250 g sorghum malt
  • 1 tsp instant yeast (optional)

Steps

  1. Bring 2500 ml water to a boil in a large pot. Slowly stir in 500 g maize meal to avoid lumps, then simmer, stirring often, until it becomes a thick porridge.
  2. Remove from heat ad let the porridge cool to lukewarm. It must not be hot or it will kill the malt enzymes and yeast.
  3. Stir in 250 g sorghum malt and the 1 tsp instant yeast (optional), mixing well. Transfer to a clean, food-safe bucket or container.
  4. Cover loosely with a cloth or lid so gas can escape. Keep in a warm spot and stir once or twice a day until bubbly and pleasantly sour, about 2 to 3 days.
  5. Strain through a clean cloth or fine sieve, squeezing out the liquid. Chill, shake well and serve.

NOTES

Best drunk within 3 to 5 days, as it keeps fermenting. Shake or stir before serving since it settles. Use very clean equipment to avoid bad fermentation, and discard it if it smells rotten or shows mould.

This recipe is a quick home version that makes about 8 servings.
